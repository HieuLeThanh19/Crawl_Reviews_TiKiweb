HUONG DAN CAP NHAT AI GEMINI CHO TIKI REVIEW SYSTEM
Ngay tao: 2026-05-15

1. TINH NANG DA THEM

- Box "Tro ly AI tong hop danh gia" nam ben duoi khu vuc ten san pham/product_id, truoc cac thong ke review.
- Nut "Tao tom tat" goi Gemini de doc review local da crawl va tra ve:
  + ket luan chung ve san pham
  + diem manh san pham
  + diem can luu y/rui ro
  + nhan xet ve giao hang/dich vu
  + loi khuyen mua hang can bang
- Hop chat noi "Tro ly AI" o goc phai man hinh.
- Chat dung Google Gemini that, co context gom thong tin san pham, thong ke sao va review da crawl.
- Neu hoi ve thong so/chat luong/giao hang/danh gia, AI se dua tren review local va thong tin san pham.
- Neu hoi kien thuc chung nhu ngay gio, AI co the tra loi ngan gon.
- Neu hoi qua xa chu de san pham, AI duoc prompt de tu choi lich su va huong ve san pham.

2. FILE DA CAP NHAT

- api/app.py
  + Them ChatMessage, ChatRequest.
  + Them helper goi Gemini bang GEMINI_API_KEY.
  + Them endpoint GET /api/products/{product_id}/ai-summary.
  + Them endpoint POST /api/chat.

- ui/dashboard.html
  + Them CSS cho box tom tat AI.
  + Them HTML box tom tat AI o panel san pham/review.
  + Them CSS/HTML/JS cho hop chat "Tro ly AI".
  + Sau khi crawl xong, dashboard tu load review va goi AI tom tat.

- requirements.txt
  + Them google-generativeai==0.8.5.

- .env.example
  + Them GEMINI_API_KEY va GEMINI_MODEL.

3. CAI THU VIEN

Chay lenh:

pip install -r requirements.txt

Neu chi muon cai rieng thu vien AI:

pip install google-generativeai==0.8.5

4. CAU HINH GEMINI API KEY

Mo file .env trong thu muc project va them:

GEMINI_API_KEY=YOUR_REAL_GEMINI_API_KEY
GEMINI_MODEL=gemini-1.5-flash

Luu y bao mat:
- Khong commit file .env len Git.
- File .env da nam trong .gitignore.
- Neu key da bi lo trong anh/chat, nen vao Google AI Studio tao key moi va xoa key cu.

5. CHAY SERVER

Vi du theo README cua project:

uvicorn api.app:app --reload --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8010

Mo trinh duyet:

http://127.0.0.1:8010

6. CACH DUNG TREN GIAO DIEN

1. Nhap ten san pham, link Tiki hoac product_id.
2. Chon dung san pham.
3. Bam "Crawl review".
4. Khi crawl xong, box "Tro ly AI tong hop danh gia" se tu goi Gemini va hien review chung.
5. Bam nut tron "AI" goc phai de mo chat.
6. Hoi vi du:
   - San pham nay co dang mua khong?
   - Review noi gi ve do ben?
   - Co ai phan nan ve giao hang khong?
   - Thong so/gia/sao cua san pham nay la gi?
   - Bay gio la may gio?

7. TEST API TRUC TIEP

Test tom tat AI:

curl http://127.0.0.1:8010/api/products/PRODUCT_ID/ai-summary

Test chat AI:

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8010/api/chat ^
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" ^
  -d "{\"product_id\":\"PRODUCT_ID\",\"history\":[],\"message\":\"San pham nay co dang mua khong?\"}"

8. CAC LOI THUONG GAP

- "Chua cau hinh GEMINI_API_KEY trong file .env"
  + Them GEMINI_API_KEY vao .env va khoi dong lai server.

- "Chua cai thu vien google-generativeai"
  + Chay pip install google-generativeai==0.8.5.

- "Chua co review local"
  + Can bam Crawl review truoc, vi AI tong hop dua tren review da luu trong SQLite.

- Gemini bao loi quota/rate limit
  + Doi mot luc roi goi lai, hoac kiem tra han muc Google AI Studio.

9. GHI CHU KY THUAT

- AI khong chi doi chieu text don gian. Backend gui prompt gom thong tin san pham, thong ke local va review that da crawl sang Gemini.
- AI duoc yeu cau suy luan can than, noi ro khi review chua du thong tin, va khong bia thong so khong co trong context.
- Endpoint chat khong co auth/rate limit. Neu public app ra internet, nen them gioi han request de tranh ton quota Gemini.
